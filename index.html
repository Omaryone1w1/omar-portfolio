01
Real-time 3D AI tutor
Qwen · Groq · Coqui TTS

Full-Stack AI · Graduation Project — in progress

AANG

A real-time 3D AI tutor: a Three.js avatar in a live classroom environment, backed by a local Qwen LLM (with Groq as a fast cloud fallback) and self-hosted Coqui TTS for voice. Built on Flask, with lip-sync, gesture animation, and a multilingual RAG-powered whiteboard — the retrieval layer has stayed the most reliable part of the system, holding up accurately across languages.

Three.jsFlaskRAGCoqui TTS

Most of the recent work has gone into a custom in-scene "debug mode" for nudging the avatar, whiteboard, and camera into place (live, on X/Y/Z) across very different environments — plus a crash course in Blender to rig and clean up avatars that don't arrive animation-ready.

02

Applied AI · Hackathon — 2nd place

Aang
2D Avatar

Built with team Blue Whales at the Helwan Cyber Arena Hackathon (organized by Cyshield & EG-CERT): a RAG-powered avatar that teaches kids AI in six languages, with an anime-styled face, synthesized speech, and Wave2Lip lip-sync — placing 2nd among 36 Egyptian universities.

RAGWave2LipText-to-Speech

The AI wasn't the hard part — Wave2Lip was. It only runs on a stack of older, specific dependencies that fought with the rest of the environment, and the pipeline was new to all of us going in, so untangling both before the demo even ran took as long as building the feature itself.
